How every number is computed
Unbilled
closed work_orders with no invoice: parts at part_prices, time_entries × rate card, expenses
Covered
agreements drawdown — the same function that draws the contract page today
Viewed
the share_links open event; no second tracking mechanism
Late fee
policy per org (grace, rate, applies-to); posts as its own line, never edits the original
Credit hold
oldest open invoice > policy days, or balance > policy amount — checked when a WO opens
Balance
always a sum over invoice_lines + fees − payments; no stored balance anywhere
